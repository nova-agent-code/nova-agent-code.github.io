Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

1. Overview

Nova Agent is a local-first desktop application. We built it so that your source code, prompts, API keys, and project files never leave your machine except when you explicitly direct them to an LLM provider of your choosing.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account, email, or identity information — Nova Agent requires no sign-up.
  • No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or usage metrics.
  • No upload of your code, prompts, file contents, or terminal output to our servers.
  • No tracking cookies. This website sets none.

3. Data that stays on your device

Your API keys, chat history, workspace settings, and session logs are stored locally in your user profile. You may delete them at any time by removing the application's data folder.

4. Third-party LLM providers

When you configure an API key, requests are sent directly from your computer to that provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or a local model). Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies. We never see, store, or proxy those requests.

5. Downloads

Installers and the version manifest are served statically from this website. Your IP address may be recorded transiently by the standard server access logs of our web host for security and bandwidth purposes only.

6. Children

Nova Agent is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

Questions? Open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the address listed on the project's README.
