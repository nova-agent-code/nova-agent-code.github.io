How to install the update

  1. 1

    Download

    Click the button above and save the .exe installer to your computer.

  2. 2

    Run the .exe

    Double-click the downloaded installer. Windows may ask you to confirm — choose Run.

  3. 3

    Auto-swap

    The installer auto-closes your old app, replaces the files, and relaunches Nova Agent on the new version.

Save your work before updating — the installer will close any running Nova Agent instance. Your settings, keys, and history are preserved.
